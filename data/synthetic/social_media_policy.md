# Sheares Hall JCRC — Social Media Policy

**Maintained by:** Director of Communications
**Last Revised:** Semester 1, AY2025/2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the use of all official Sheares Hall social media accounts, including Instagram, Telegram broadcast channels, Facebook pages, and any future platforms adopted by the JCRC.

---

## 2. Resident Privacy

2.1 No post on any official hall account may include **personally identifiable information** about residents (including but not limited to full names, room numbers, matriculation numbers, phone numbers, or email addresses) without the **explicit written consent** of the individual(s) concerned.

2.2 Event photos that incidentally capture residents in public hall spaces may be posted, but must not single out any individual by name or caption without consent.

2.3 Any resident who wishes to be removed from a posted photo or caption must notify the Director of Communications, and the content will be amended or taken down within **24 hours**.

---

## 3. Approval and Publishing

3.1 All posts on official JCRC accounts must be **reviewed and approved by the Director of Communications** (or an appointed Communications Officer) before publication.

3.2 Time-sensitive announcements (e.g. event day reminders) may be expedited, but must still receive sign-off from the Director of Communications or the President.

3.3 No JCRC member, sub-committee lead, or sports convenor may publish independently to official accounts without prior approval.

---

## 4. Sponsorship Acknowledgement

4.1 When a sponsor provides funding or in-kind support for a hall event, the sponsor's logo or acknowledgement must appear in at least one official post **within 24 hours of funds or goods being received**.

4.2 Sponsorship acknowledgements must conform to the branding guidelines provided by the sponsor and must not be altered without the sponsor's approval.

---

## 5. Conduct and Tone

5.1 Official accounts must not be used to air **personal grievances**, comment on internal disputes, or post content that could embarrass the hall or its residents.

5.2 All content must comply with the university's Acceptable Use Policy and applicable laws.

5.3 Violations of this policy may result in removal from communications responsibilities and referral to the JCRC for disciplinary review.

---

## 6. Branding

6.1 All posts must use the approved **Sheares Hall JCRC visual identity** — including official colour scheme, fonts, and logo — as specified in the JCRC Branding Guideline document maintained by the Director of Communications.
