# Sheares Hall — Inter-Hall Games (IHG) Participation Rules

**Maintained by:** Director of Sports
**Last Revised:** AY2025/2026

---

## 1. Purpose

Inter-Hall Games (IHG) is a university-wide inter-hall competition spanning multiple sports. This document sets out the rules governing Sheares Hall's participation, including eligibility, attendance, forfeit conditions, and dispute resolution.

---

## 2. Team Eligibility

2.1 Each sport entered by Sheares Hall must have a registered squad of **at least 5 hall residents**.

2.2 All squad members must be current, full-time residents of Sheares Hall for the duration of the IHG season.

2.3 Squad registration lists must be submitted to the Director of Sports and ratified by the DRH office before the IHG registration deadline set by the university.

---

## 3. Training Attendance

3.1 Registered squad members are expected to attend a minimum of **60% of all scheduled training sessions** for their sport to remain eligible to participate in official IHG matches.

3.2 Attendance is tracked by the sport convenor using the official training register.

3.3 Members who fall below the 60% threshold due to **documented extenuating circumstances** (e.g. medical leave, academic examinations) may appeal to the Director of Sports for a waiver. The Director's decision is final.

---

## 4. Match Forfeit Rules

4.1 A team that cannot field the minimum number of players (as set by the IHG governing rules for each sport) at the scheduled match time will be recorded as a **forfeit**.

4.2 Forfeited matches will result in a 0-3 loss (or equivalent default score) being recorded for Sheares Hall.

4.3 Convenors must notify the opposing hall's convenor and the Director of Sports at least **4 hours before match time** if a forfeit is anticipated.

---

## 5. Convenership Responsibilities

5.1 Each sport shall have an appointed **Sport Convenor** who is responsible for scheduling, communication, and match-day logistics.

5.2 When Sheares Hall is the **hosting hall** for a match, the convenor is responsible for:
- Booking and preparing the venue to the standard required by the sport.
- Providing a qualified or experienced referee, or coordinating with the opposing hall to arrange one.
- Ensuring spectator areas are clearly separated from the playing area.

5.3 The convenor shall submit a post-match report to the Director of Sports within 48 hours of each match.

---

## 6. Disputes

6.1 Disputes arising during or after a match (including disputes over scores, eligibility, or referee decisions) must be raised in writing to the **IHG Committee** within **48 hours** of the match conclusion.

6.2 The IHG Committee (comprised of Director of Sports representatives from all participating halls) will review and issue a ruling within 48 hours of receiving the dispute.

6.3 Rulings by the IHG Committee are final and binding.
