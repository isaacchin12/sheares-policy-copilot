# Sheares Hall — Shearite Points System

**Maintained by:** Director of Block Welfare & Director of Sports
**Last Revised:** AY2025/2026

---

## 1. Purpose

The Shearite Points System (SPS) tracks resident participation in hall life and informs room retention priority. Residents who contribute more actively to the community are given priority in room allocation for the following semester.

---

## 2. Points Accrual

Residents earn points by attending or participating in sessions run by the following committees:

| Activity Category | Points per Session |
|---|---|
| Block Committee (Block Comm) sessions | 3 points |
| Culture Committee events | 3 points |
| Sports Committee training / matches | 3 points |
| Communications Committee initiatives | 2 points |

2.1 Points are awarded per confirmed attendance, tracked by sub-committee leads using the official attendance register.

2.2 Residents who sign in but leave before the session ends (as determined by the sub-committee lead) will not receive points for that session.

2.3 Points from multiple categories are cumulative within a semester.

---

## 3. Supplementary Points

3.1 Residents who make **extraordinary contributions** to hall life — such as taking on a convenorship, organising a major event independently, or providing sustained welfare support — may be nominated by a JCRC Director for supplementary points.

3.2 Supplementary points are capped at **10 additional points per resident per semester**.

3.3 All supplementary point awards must be approved by the President and Director of Block Welfare and recorded in the official points ledger.

---

## 4. Mandatory Minimum

4.1 Residents must accumulate a **minimum of 30 points** in a semester to be eligible for room retention priority in the following semester's allocation.

4.2 Points are counted per semester. They do not carry over from previous semesters.

---

## 5. Retention Priority Tiers

Points are used to assign room allocation priority tiers as follows:

| Tier | Points Range | Priority Level |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 80 points and above | Priority A — First choice in Round 1 ballot |
| Tier 2 | 50–79 points | Priority B — Second choice in Round 1 ballot |
| Tier 3 | 30–49 points | Priority C — Third choice in Round 1 ballot |
| Ineligible | Below 30 points | Not eligible for priority room retention |

---

## 6. Points Release and Appeals

6.1 The Director of Block Welfare shall release the provisional points tally to all residents **within 2 weeks of the last day of the semester**.

6.2 Residents who believe their points have been recorded incorrectly may submit an **appeal in writing** to the Director of Block Welfare within **2 weeks of the points release date**.

6.3 Appeals must include supporting evidence (e.g. sub-committee sign-in sheets, event photos). Appeals without supporting evidence will not be reviewed.

6.4 The Director of Block Welfare's decision on appeals is final and will be communicated within 1 week of the appeal window closing.
