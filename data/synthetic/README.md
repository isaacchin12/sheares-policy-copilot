# Synthetic Policy Documents — Portfolio Demo

This directory contains **synthetic, illustrative policy documents** created for the Sheares Policy Copilot portfolio project.

## Important Disclaimer

These documents are **entirely fictional**. They were written to demonstrate a RAG-based policy Q&A system and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representative of any real university, residential hall, or student organisation.

Specifically:
- All rules, figures, deadlines, and procedures are invented for demonstration purposes.
- No real names, matriculation numbers, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personally identifiable information appear anywhere in these documents.
- The structure and terminology are inspired by typical hall governance documents but do not reflect the actual policies of Sheares Hall at the National University of Singapore or any other institution.

## Files in this Directory

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `constitution.md` | Fictional JCRC constitution — membership eligibility, committee structure, quorum and amendment rules |
| `finance_procedures.md` | Fictional finance procedures — budget timelines, claim rules, receipt requirements, reimbursements |
| `social_media_policy.md` | Fictional social media policy — resident privacy, post approval, sponsorship acknowledgement |
| `points_system.md` | Fictional Shearite Points System — accrual rates, tiers, retention priority, appeals |
| `subsidies.md` | Fictional holiday subsidy procedures — eligibility, rates, application deadlines, payment |
| `room_allocation.md` | Fictional room allocation procedures — priority rounds, room types, swaps, medical exemptions |
| `ihg_rules.md` | Fictional IHG participation rules — eligibility, attendance, forfeits, disputes |
| `events_sop.md` | Fictional events SOP — risk assessment, venue booking, welfare caps, post-event debrief |

## Purpose

These documents serve as a knowledge base for testing and demonstrating a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline that answers natural-language questions about hall policies. The goal is to show how an AI assistant can accurately surface specific policy details (e.g. "What is the deadline to submit a subsidy application?") from a corpus of structured documents.
