# Sheares Hall — Room Allocation Procedures

**Maintained by:** Dean of Residential Housing (DRH) Office in coordination with JCRC President
**Last Revised:** AY2025/2026

---

## 1. Overview

Room allocation for each semester is conducted in two rounds. Returning residents are prioritised based on their Shearite Points tier from the preceding semester (see `points_system.md`). Freshmen and new residents enter allocation through a separate process managed by the DRH office.

---

## 2. Room Types

| Room Type | Description | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Single Standard | Single occupancy, standard furnishing | Base rate |
| Single Premium | Single occupancy, larger floor area or preferred block/level | +$50 surcharge per semester |
| Double | Shared occupancy, two residents per room | Reduced rate; both occupants must apply jointly |

---

## 3. Round 1 — Priority Ballot

3.1 Round 1 is open only to returning residents who meet the minimum 30-point threshold and have been assigned a retention priority tier (Tier 1, 2, or 3).

3.2 Residents select room preferences in the following order:
- **Tier 1 (Priority A)** residents select first, in random order within the tier.
- **Tier 2 (Priority B)** residents select next, in random order within the tier.
- **Tier 3 (Priority C)** residents select last, in random order within the tier.

3.3 Selections are made via the hall's online room selection portal during the designated window communicated by the DRH office.

---

## 4. Round 2 — Open Ballot

4.1 Any rooms remaining after Round 1 — including rooms vacated by non-returning residents and rooms declined in Round 1 — will be made available in Round 2.

4.2 Round 2 is open to all eligible applicants, including returning residents who did not qualify for priority retention and incoming residents not yet allocated.

4.3 Allocation in Round 2 is by **random ballot** with no priority ordering.

---

## 5. Premium Room Surcharge

5.1 Residents who select a Single Premium room will be charged an additional **$50.00 per semester** on top of the standard hall fee.

5.2 This surcharge is non-refundable once the room selection is confirmed.

---

## 6. Room Swaps

6.1 Residents may request a room swap with another resident within the **first 2 weeks of the semester**.

6.2 Both parties must consent and submit a Room Swap Request Form to the hall office. Swaps are subject to DRH approval.

6.3 No room swaps will be approved after the 2-week window except under extraordinary circumstances at the DRH's discretion.

---

## 7. Medical Exemptions

7.1 Residents with documented medical conditions requiring specific room types (e.g. ground floor access, proximity to facilities) should submit a Medical Exemption Request to the DRH office **before** the Round 1 ballot opens.

7.2 Medical exemptions are handled directly by the DRH and are not subject to the standard points-based priority system.
