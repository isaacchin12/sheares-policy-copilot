# Sheares Hall JCRC — Finance Procedures

**Maintained by:** Director of Finance
**Last Revised:** Semester 1, AY2025/2026

---

## 1. Budget Submission

1.1 All sub-committees and sports/culture groups must submit their **proposed budget for the semester** to the Director of Finance no later than the **last Friday of Month 1** of each semester.

1.2 Budget proposals must use the official **Event Budget Template** (available on the JCRC shared drive). Submissions in any other format will be returned without processing.

1.3 The Director of Finance will consolidate proposals and present a consolidated hall budget to the JCRC for approval within **2 weeks** of the submission deadline.

---

## 2. Claim Submissions

2.1 All reimbursement claims for approved expenses must be submitted to the Director of Finance **within 30 calendar days** of the event or purchase date.

2.2 Late submissions will not be processed unless accompanied by a written explanation approved by the President.

2.3 Claims must be submitted via the official **Claim Form**, available from the Finance folder on the JCRC shared drive.

---

## 3. Receipt Requirements

3.1 **All expenditure of $10.00 or more** must be supported by an **original receipt**. Photocopies or digital screenshots are not accepted unless the vendor does not issue physical receipts, in which case a statutory declaration by the claimant is required.

3.2 Receipts for amounts below $10.00 are encouraged but not mandatory; a written declaration of expenditure is sufficient.

3.3 Receipts must clearly show the vendor name, date, itemised breakdown, and total amount paid.

---

## 4. Petty Cash

4.1 A petty cash float of up to **$100 per event** may be requested in advance from the Director of Finance.

4.2 Petty cash must be reconciled and returned (with receipts) within **5 working days** of the event.

4.3 Unreturned or unreconciled petty cash will be treated as a personal liability of the requesting member.

---

## 5. Reimbursement Processing

5.1 Approved claims will be processed and disbursed within **10 working days** of submission.

5.2 Payment will be made via bank transfer to the claimant's registered bank account. Cash reimbursements are not issued except in exceptional circumstances approved by the Director of Finance and President.

---

## 6. Sponsorship Income

6.1 All sponsorship income received by any JCRC sub-committee must be declared to the Director of Finance within **3 working days** of receipt.

6.2 Sponsorship funds must be deposited into the hall's designated account and may only be drawn down once recorded in the finance ledger.

6.3 Sponsors must be issued an official receipt or acknowledgement letter signed by the Director of Finance and the President.

---

## 7. Audit and Transparency

7.1 The Director of Finance shall produce a **semester-end financial report** for presentation at the final General Meeting of each semester.

7.2 All residents are entitled to request a summary of hall expenditure for the current semester by writing to the Director of Finance.
