# Sheares Hall Junior Common Room Committee — Constitution

**Effective Date:** Academic Year 2025/2026
**Maintained by:** Secretary-General, JCRC

---

## 1. Name and Purpose

The governing body of Sheares Hall residents shall be known as the **Junior Common Room Committee (JCRC)**. Its purpose is to represent the interests of all hall residents, organise community events, and uphold the values of the Sheares Hall community.

---

## 2. Membership and Eligibility

2.1 All residents currently residing in Sheares Hall are considered **Ordinary Members** of the Junior Common Room (JCR).

2.2 To be eligible for a **JCRC committee role**, a candidate must:
- Have completed or be completing at least **60 weeks of cumulative residency** in Sheares Hall by the date of election.
- Be a registered full-time student at the university for the duration of the term.
- Not be serving a disciplinary suspension at the time of nomination.

2.3 Freshmen in their first semester of residency are not eligible to stand for JCRC positions but may vote in all elections.

---

## 3. JCRC Structure

The JCRC shall consist of the following seven positions:

| Position | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| President | Overall leadership; liaises with Dean of Residential Housing (DRH) |
| Director of Finance | Budget oversight and financial reporting |
| Director of Communications | Internal and external communications, social media |
| Director of Culture | Cultural events and inter-hall relations |
| Director of Sports | Sports events and IHG coordination |
| Director of Block Welfare | Block-level welfare and resident support |
| Director of Logistics | Venue, equipment, and operations |

---

## 4. Quorum

4.1 A quorum for any JCRC meeting shall require the attendance of at least **two-thirds (2/3) of all committee members**.

4.2 Decisions made without quorum are not binding and must be ratified at the next quorate meeting.

---

## 5. Amendment Procedures

5.1 Amendments to this constitution may be proposed by any JCRC member.

5.2 A proposed amendment must be circulated in writing to all JCRC members at least **7 days** before the vote.

5.3 Amendments are passed upon receiving at least a **three-quarters (3/4) supermajority** of votes from all JCRC members (not merely those present).

5.4 Ratified amendments take effect from the start of the following academic semester unless otherwise stated.
