# Sheares Hall JCRC — Events Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

**Maintained by:** Director of Logistics
**Last Revised:** AY2025/2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This SOP ensures all Sheares Hall events are planned safely, approved properly, and documented consistently. It applies to all events organised by the JCRC, sub-committees, sports teams, and culture groups operating under the JCRC umbrella.

---

## 2. Risk Assessment

2.1 A completed **Risk Assessment Form** must be submitted to the Director of Logistics **at least 2 weeks before the event date**.

2.2 The Risk Assessment Form must identify foreseeable hazards, affected parties, likelihood and severity ratings, and proposed mitigation measures.

2.3 Events involving physical activity, water, fire, heights, or external participants are classified as **high-risk** and require additional sign-off from the JCRC President before proceeding.

---

## 3. External Participants

3.1 Any event that includes **non-residents (external participants)** — including guests from other halls, university clubs, or the public — must receive approval from the **Office of Student Affairs (OSA)** before the event is confirmed.

3.2 The event organiser must submit an OSA event approval request, including estimated external attendance, venue details, and a copy of the Risk Assessment Form, at least **3 weeks before the event**.

3.3 Approved external participant numbers must not be exceeded on the day of the event.

---

## 4. Venue Booking

4.1 All venue bookings within Sheares Hall must be made through the **hall office** with a minimum of **72 hours' notice**.

4.2 Bookings are subject to availability and may be declined if the space has been reserved by the DRH office or for maintenance.

4.3 Organisers are responsible for the condition of the venue. Any damage caused during the event will be charged to the relevant sub-committee's budget.

---

## 5. Welfare Budget

5.1 The welfare budget (food, beverages, and care packs for participants) is capped at **$5.00 per head** per event.

5.2 Any welfare spend exceeding this cap must be pre-approved by the Director of Finance and the JCRC President.

5.3 All welfare expenditure must be claimed within 30 days of the event in accordance with the Finance Procedures.

---

## 6. External Vendor Approval

6.1 External vendors (caterers, equipment suppliers, performers) engaged for any hall event must be approved by the Director of Logistics **at least 1 week before the event**.

6.2 The organiser must provide a vendor quote or invoice to the Director of Finance for budget alignment before approval is granted.

6.3 Payments to external vendors must be processed through the Director of Finance and may not be made directly by individual members without prior written authorisation.

---

## 7. Post-Event Debrief

7.1 The event organiser must submit a **Post-Event Debrief Report** to the Director of Logistics within **1 week of the event's conclusion**.

7.2 The report must cover: attendance figures, budget actuals vs. estimates, key outcomes, feedback received, and recommendations for future editions.

7.3 Reports are retained in the JCRC shared drive and used to inform planning for subsequent events and semesters.
