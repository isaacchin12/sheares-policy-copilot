# Sheares Hall — Holiday Subsidy Procedures

**Maintained by:** Director of Finance
**Last Revised:** AY2025/2026

---

## 1. Overview

Sheares Hall offers a **Summer Holiday Subsidy** to eligible residents who are approved to remain in the hall during the vacation period between semesters. The subsidy is intended to reduce the accommodation cost for residents with academic or other approved commitments during the break.

---

## 2. Eligibility

To qualify for the summer subsidy, a resident must:

2.1 Be an approved **vacation stay resident** — i.e. have received written confirmation from the Dean of Residential Housing (DRH) office that their vacation stay application has been accepted.

2.2 Have accumulated **at least 30 Shearite Points** in the preceding semester (per the Shearite Points System).

2.3 Have submitted a complete subsidy application **before the deadline** (see Section 3). Late applications will not be considered.

---

## 3. Application Deadline

3.1 The subsidy application window closes on the **last Friday of the exam period** of the preceding semester.

3.2 Applications must be submitted using the official Subsidy Application Form, available from the Finance folder on the JCRC shared drive.

3.3 Incomplete applications (missing vacation stay approval letter or points confirmation) will be rejected and may not be resubmitted.

---

## 4. Subsidy Rates

Subsidy amounts are calculated based on the total number of approved vacation stay days:

| Duration of Stay | Subsidy Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–7 days | $8.00 per day |
| 8–14 days | $7.00 per day |
| More than 14 days | $6.00 per day |

4.1 The applicable rate is determined by the **total approved duration**, not on a tiered basis. For example, a resident staying 10 days receives $7.00 per day for all 10 days.

4.2 Subsidy amounts are paid as a **one-time lump sum** after the vacation period, not in advance.

---

## 5. Payment

5.1 Approved subsidy payments will be disbursed **within 3 weeks of the start of the following semester**.

5.2 Payment is made via bank transfer to the resident's registered bank account. Residents must ensure their bank details are up to date in the hall office records.

5.3 Residents who vacate the hall before the end of their approved stay period will have their subsidy **pro-rated** to the actual number of days stayed, as verified by hall office records.
